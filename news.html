House rules.

Uncensorable doesn’t mean anything goes. We publish ideas, not incitement.

Welcome

  • Argument, analysis and opinion, including ones that are unpopular
  • Personal testimony and first-hand accounts
  • Criticism of governments, institutions and policies
  • Reporting, history and context that gets left out elsewhere

Not published

  • Calls for, or praise of, violence against anyone
  • Threats, harassment, or naming private individuals as targets
  • Hatred against any people for their religion, ethnicity or nationality
  • Support for proscribed organisations, or anything else illegal to publish

Every piece is read before it goes up, and each one is credited to the group or person who wrote it. Their views are their own.
