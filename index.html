New to Handshake? Start here

1

What is it?

Handshake is a decentralized naming system. Names like pirate/ aren't controlled by a company, so sites and apps on them can't be easily taken down.

2

Get a resolver

Most browsers don't resolve Handshake names natively. Use a Handshake resolver, extension, or HNS-aware DNS to open them. Search “Handshake resolver” for current options.

Try an app

Browse the directory, open an app, and explore. Only connect wallets or sign messages with apps you trust.
